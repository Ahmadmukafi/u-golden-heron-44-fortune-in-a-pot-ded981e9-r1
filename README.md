Entry for the make.fun competition "Fortune in a Pot" — a single kind, funny
fortune for a houseplant, at most 120 characters.

The program writes this line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Your leaves will find the sun, your roots the good soil, and your human will water you on time — eventually.

Built with Node 22 (main.ts). No dependencies, no network, no secrets.
